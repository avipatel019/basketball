Our story

Born from one quiet evening

Ember & Oak started in a tiny kitchen, with a single pot of melted soy wax and a stubborn belief that a candle should do more than smell nice — it should change the temperature of a whole room.

Today we still pour every candle by hand, blending fragrance oils with natural essential oils until each scent feels honest, layered, and unmistakably ours. No shortcuts, no synthetic harshness — just warmth you can light.
